Accessibility Statement

Last updated: [publication date to be added]

Senior Life Navigator is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including seniors and people with visual, motor, or cognitive disabilities — because our clients and their families are exactly who this site is built for.

What We've Done

  • Used Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for readability by people with low vision;
  • Kept body text at a minimum of 16px with generous line spacing;
  • Maintained strong colour contrast between text and backgrounds;
  • Made sure the site can be navigated using a keyboard alone, with visible focus indicators;
  • Provided text alternatives for icons and interactive elements;
  • Respected reduced-motion preferences for anyone sensitive to on-screen movement.

Ongoing Work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. If you experience any difficulty using this website, please let us know — we want to fix it.

Let Us Know

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site, email info@seniorlifenavigator.ca or call (519) 804-6141, and we'll do our best to assist you directly and address the issue.
