FOR INDEPENDENT SERVICE PROVIDERS

Become a Referral Partner

Senior Life Navigator regularly coordinates trusted, practical support for seniors and families across Waterloo Region — home cleaning, yard care, snow removal, minor repairs, moving/downsizing help, and more. If your business serves this community well, we'd like to hear from you.

This is a referral relationship, not employment or subcontracting. You remain an independent business, you're paid directly by the client, and you retain full control over how you do your work. A written Referral Agreement is signed before any formal referrals begin.

Who we're looking for

🧹 House cleaning & organization
🌿 Yard care & snow removal
🔧 Minor home repairs & handyman services
📦 Moving & downsizing support
🐾 Pet care
🚗 Transportation & driving services

Don't see your service listed? Reach out anyway — we work with a range of local providers.

How it works

  • Submit your information using the form below. This is an expression of interest, not a contract.
  • We review your business — insurance, any required certifications, and references.
  • We sign a simple Referral Agreement together, setting out the referral fee and terms in writing.
  • We refer clients your way when a good fit comes up. You invoice and are paid directly by the client — we're never in the middle of that transaction.

Tell us about your business

Senior Life Navigator refers clients based on independent judgment and does not guarantee referral volume. All referred providers operate as independent businesses, not as employees, agents, or subcontractors of Senior Life Navigator.
